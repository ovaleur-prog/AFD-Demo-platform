# Design System

## 1. Purpose
Defines the visual identity, UI components and interaction rules for the Solar Grid Monitoring Dashboard.

Inspired by AREAS & WeWebBox; it should communicate:
- Solar energy
- Innovation & sustainability
- African infrastructure
- Operational excellence
- Accessibility & reliability
- Digital industry

Professional, modern and data-driven, yet simple for non-technical users.

## 2. Design Principles

### Energy at the Centre
Prioritize production and infrastructure performance. Keep key metrics immediately visible:
- Current solar production
- Installed capacity & network availability
- Active sites & operational alerts
- Environmental impact

### Clear & Accessible
Short labels, clear spacing, simple charts, explicit status indicators, contextual explanations and progressive disclosure. Avoid technical overload.

### Impact-Oriented
Connect operations to impact: CO₂ avoided, households connected, renewable energy produced and communities served.

### African & Locally Grounded
Keep real regions, local contexts and solar infrastructure central to the experience.

### Digital Sovereignty
Use optimized images, lightweight components, limited animation, a restrained palette, clear data hierarchy and simple page structures.

## 3. Colour System

### Solar Yellow
Solar energy, electricity and highlighted actions.
- Daylight: `#F9D648`
- Hover: `#E6B920`
- Active: `#A78512`
- Light: `#FFF6D1`

### Energy Green
Sustainability, positive performance and environmental impact.
- Daylight: `#73C66E`
- Hover: `#5AAE55`
- Dark: `#2F7A3A`
- Light: `#E8F5E9`

### Deep Navy
Technology, reliability and industrial credibility.
- Daylight: `#0F172A`
- Hover: `#1E293B`
- Dark: `#020617`
- Light: `#E2E8F0`

### Semantic Colours
- Info: `#3B82F6`
- Warning: `#F59E0B`
- Critical: `#DC2626`
- Offline: `#71717A`

### Neutrals
- Page background: `#F8FAFC`
- Text: `#0F172A`
- Surface / Cards: `#FFFFFF`
- Lines / Borders: `#E2E8F0`

## 4. Typography

### Primary Font: Inter
Use Inter as the primary interface font; use a clean sans-serif fallback.
