PRD.mdWHAT

Solar Grid Monitoring Dashboard

Overview

A web application that enables energy operators to monitor solar energy production across multiple African regions.

The platform provides real-time visibility into energy generation, network performance, equipment health, and operational alerts through a modern and intuitive dashboard.

Problem Statement

Energy operators often manage multiple solar installations spread across large geographic areas.

Monitoring production, identifying anomalies, and understanding regional performance can be time-consuming when information is distributed across several systems.

A centralized dashboard is needed to simplify monitoring and improve decision-making.

Target Users

  • Grid Operators
  • Energy Analysts
  • Operations Managers
  • Executive Stakeholders

Objectives

  • Centralize operational data
  • Visualize production metrics
  • Detect anomalies quickly
  • Track asset performance
  • Support operational and strategic decisions

Success Criteria

  • Users can identify underperforming regions in less than 30 seconds
  • Users can view key production KPIs at a glance
  • Users can monitor active alerts
  • Users can compare performance across regions

Best Practices & Limits

Tips for effective vibe coding

The essentials
1
Describe the need with concrete examples: who will use the app, in which situation, and what result is expected.
2
One feature or change at a time; say what must change and what must stay the same.
3
Read and confirm the business summary and scope before asking for the build.
4
Give feedback on the visual prototype before asking for the real features.
5
Test with representative examples, then explain precisely what looks wrong.
6
Ask for a clear report of the checks performed and the remaining open points.
Never share a password, token or key in a conversation or in the code.
Advanced tips
Match the model to the taskAdapt the model and reasoning effort to the task's complexity. If Auto mode isn't good enough, explicitly pick a more capable model, and follow your team's recommendations when they exist.
Be preciseGive context, users, constraints, examples and the expected result. You can ask the AI to improve your initial prompt, then check it matches your need and contains no secrets.
Stuck after several exchanges?Ask for a full diagnosis, a resolution plan, then its implementation. Raise the model level and reasoning effort if needed.
Attach a screenshotIllustrate a problem when useful, and mask tokens, personal data and confidential information.

Keep Your Conversations Focused

Give each chat one clear goal and only the context it needs.

01
One feature or bug, one conversation. Start a new thread when the goal changes, such as moving from a login form to CI/CD configuration.
02
Set the context explicitly. At the start of each thread, reference only the relevant code with #file, #folder or @workspace.
03
Rename and archive your chats. Use your extension's chat history to keep active sessions clearly named and easy to find.
04
Restart when you're stuck. If the AI loops or repeats the same mistake, start a fresh thread and explain the problem again with only the relevant code.
